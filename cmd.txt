If a flight goes:

Takeoff → climbs from 0 to 8,000 ft → 🟢 Green
    - Continues climbing to 20,000 ft → 🟡 Yellow
    - Reaches cruising at 35,000 ft → 🔴 Red
    - Descends back through 20,000 ft → 🟡 Yellow
    - Lands below 10,000 ft → 🟢 Green
